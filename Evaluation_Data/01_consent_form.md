# Participant Information and Consent Form
**Study:** Usability of "Day Shift: The Painter", a game for gentle arm exercise
**Course:** CS 435 Human-Computer Interaction, IIT Gandhinagar (student course project)
**Researchers:** Akshat A. Nishad, Rudra P. Singh, Laksh Jain, Harshil H Shah
**Supervisor:** Dr. Yogesh K. Meena

## What is this study about?
We are testing a computer game prototype. In the game, you "paint" walls by moving your arm in front of a webcam. We want to learn whether it is easy to understand, comfortable and respectful to use. **We are testing the game, not you.**

## What will I do?
- The session takes about **20–25 minutes**.
- You will sit in front of a laptop and do a few short tasks: get set up, follow a short training, paint one or two walls, pause, and change a setting.
- You will be asked to "think aloud" (say what you are thinking).
- At the end there is a short questionnaire.
- The arm movements are gentle and done **seated**. You choose how far to move.

## Risks and how we reduce them
| Possible risk | What we do |
|---|---|
| Tiredness or discomfort in the arm | Short session, rest breaks, seated, stop at any time. The game never rushes you. |
| Pain or dizziness | Stop immediately. A researcher sits with you the whole time. |
| Privacy (camera) | The camera picture is processed on the laptop only. **No video, photo or audio is recorded or stored.** |
| Feeling judged | There are no right or wrong answers. We are testing the design. |

## Your data
- We record only: task times, the number of errors, your questionnaire answers, the researcher's notes, and the game's numeric results (angles, scores).
- You are identified only by a code (for example **P3**). Your name is not linked to your answers.
- We do **not** collect medical history, contact details or any sensitive personal data.
- The anonymised data is used only for the course report and presentation.

## Voluntary participation
Taking part is **completely voluntary**. You may stop at any time, skip any question, or ask for your data to be removed, without giving a reason and without any consequence.
